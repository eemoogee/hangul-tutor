Inspect a piece

What is real. Each piece is a contour from Noto Sans CJK KR Bold. Closed, the pieces sit exactly where the font draws them, so the assembled syllable is the font's own 나, 노 or 난. In this font, 노 is a single joined outline: the ㄴ rests on the top of ㅗ's stem. The study cuts it along that line.

What is a visual metaphor. Lifting, thickness, shadows and tilt show the parts. They are not how Hangul is written or how it was designed, and the motion is not stroke order.

Compare. Lift keeps the letters face-on and shows depth with scale, shadow and finger parallax. Layers raises each piece toward you, outer edge first, and shows the letter's thickness. The page underneath never tilts.
