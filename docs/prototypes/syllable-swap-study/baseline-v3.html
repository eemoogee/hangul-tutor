What is real. Every shape is an outline from Noto Sans CJK KR Bold. Each syllable's letters are its own font shapes, so 노's ㄴ really is wider and lower than 나's. In six syllables (노 도 모 보 머 버) the font joins consonant and vowel into one outline; the study cuts them apart along the join and changes no outline.

What is a visual metaphor. Lifting, thickness, shadows and the shading sweep show the parts. Between two shapes, a letter stretches to its new size and fades from one outline to the other. That in-between moment is animation, not handwriting or stroke order.

나 ↔ 노. Here the ㄴ morphs point by point between the font's two forms, with no fade. The motion shows that a letter's position in the block changes its proportions. Consonants do not bend or stretch when Korean is written.
